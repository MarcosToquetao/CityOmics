## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by my seat). I read this as: the study's design, data pipeline and statistical inference must be good enough that a quantitative-criminology or spatial-statistics referee at a high-impact urban-science journal would accept each reported number as an honest answer to the question asked of it. For an observational design built on administrative police records, cadastral parcels and a household travel survey, this means the identification assumptions behind every model (here: a known-composition Poisson attribution fitted by EM, permutation nulls, variance decomposition, a staggered event study and a forecasting benchmark) are stated, defensible and probed; uncertainty is quantified at the right level of dependence (spatial and temporal clustering); multiplicity is controlled across the family of tests; power or minimum detectable effects are reported where null results are interpreted; preregistered versus exploratory analyses are distinguished; and the Methods contain enough detail for an independent team with the same data to reproduce each Result.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, domain seat). From a methodology standpoint, this dimension asks whether the substantive criminological and urban-science claims match what the field already knows and whether prior findings and terms are correctly represented. I do not score it; I will only note where a methodological defect would also create a domain misstatement, and leave the verdict to the domain seat.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, DA-owned, methodology-eligible). I read this as: the central thesis must follow from the evidence actually presented, with no step that relies on a fallacy (circular validation, ecological inference to individuals, causal language from correlational designs, treating non-rejection as confirmation). From my seat this means checking that each statistic supports the sentence that cites it, that numbers agree across abstract, main text, legends and Methods, that the chain of analyses forms one coherent story rather than a collection of loosely related results, and that the strength of each claim is calibrated to the evidential status of the analysis (development-stage versus confirmatory).

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, perspective seat). This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications are accessible and substantiated for readers in adjacent fields. Methodologically, a borrowed analogy is only acceptable if the operational definitions behind it are precise; I will not score it but may flag where an analogy outruns its operationalisation as a coherence issue under D3.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, journal-fit seat). This covers organisation, clarity, figure and table quality and venue conventions. I do not score it; methodological detail that is superfluous to the story, or missing from Methods while present in legends, may be raised by me as a reproducibility or coherence point rather than a writing verdict.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, journal-fit seat). This asks whether the manuscript suits the venue and makes an original, significant contribution. I do not score it; my methodology verdict bears on it only in that a contribution resting on unsound inference is not a contribution.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit, testable identification assumptions for the attribution model (rate proportional to exposure, homogeneity within spatial units, handling of non-geocoded or unmatched events) with sensitivity analyses; validation that is independent of the fitted attribution rather than circular; permutation nulls whose exchangeability unit matches the dependence structure being tested; goodness-of-fit tests accompanied by power or equivalence reasoning so that non-rejection is not read as confirmation; variance decompositions with stated estimands, uncertainty and order dependence; event-study designs robust to staggered-adoption bias (heterogeneity-robust estimators, pre-trend tests, explicit minimum detectable effect); forecasting benchmarks with proper scoring rules, out-of-sample splits that respect time and space, clustered resampling for intervals, and fair tuned baselines; multiplicity control across the test family; clear separation of preregistered confirmatory analyses from exploratory ones; Methods detailed enough to replicate each Result.
what_triggers_block: At least one headline result rests on an analysis whose key identification assumption is unstated or untested in a way that could plausibly reverse or substantially change the claim, or on a validation that reuses the information it is meant to test, or on inference whose uncertainty ignores evident spatial or temporal dependence, but the defect is repairable with additional analysis, reframing or disclosure within a revision.
what_triggers_warn: The core analyses are sound in design but there are reproducibility gaps in the Methods, missing sensitivity or power analyses for secondary claims, incomplete multiplicity accounting, uncertainty intervals missing for some reported estimates, or methodological detail that is superfluous to the story, none of which would on its own overturn a headline claim.
what_triggers_fatal: The central claim is produced by construction of the method itself (for example an attribution whose output is guaranteed by its input assumptions and then presented as an empirical discovery), or the data cannot in principle identify the quantity claimed, so that no revision short of a different study could support the core thesis.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Each statistic quoted in a sentence actually measures what that sentence asserts; numbers match across abstract, text, legends and Methods; the sequence of analyses builds one logically connected argument with no orphan results; claim strength is calibrated to evidential status, with development-stage results framed as provisional pending confirmation; no circular reasoning, ecological fallacy, causal language from non-causal designs, or interpretation of failure to reject as evidence for the null; the guiding analogy is backed by operational definitions rather than carrying inferential weight.
what_triggers_block: A load-bearing claim in the abstract or main conclusions is not supported by, or is contradicted by, the statistic offered for it, or the argument depends on a reasoning step such as circular validation or reading non-significance as confirmation, in a way that could be repaired by reframing claims or adding the missing link.
what_triggers_warn: The argument holds overall but some sentences overstate what their statistic shows, some numbers are inconsistent across sections, some results are tangential to the main thread, or provisional findings are occasionally phrased as settled.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis is internally contradictory or rests entirely on a fallacious inference that the presented evidence cannot repair, so that the paper's main conclusion does not follow from its own results under any reasonable reframing.
