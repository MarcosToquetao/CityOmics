## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat). From an adversarial stance, this dimension asks whether the design, data handling, statistics and reproducibility support could survive a hostile referee in the field: whether the pipeline that turns administrative records into analysable units is transparent, whether uncertainty is reported, and whether another group could rerun it. I do not score it, but any methodological weakness that breaks the argument will be reported as a finding.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat). This dimension asks whether the claims match what the relevant literatures already establish, whether prior work is represented fairly, and whether field-specific terms and results are used correctly. Adversarially, it is where borrowed vocabulary can be misapplied or where established findings can be mis-stated as new. I do not score it; I report misrepresentations only when they bear on the core argument.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by me as Devil's Advocate; also eligible for the methodology seat). This is my dimension: whether the central thesis holds together from title to final sentence, whether each claimed conclusion is actually entailed by the evidence offered rather than by relabelling or analogy, whether sections contradict each other, and whether rival explanations that are more parsimonious have been considered and excluded. A paper passes only if the strongest counter-argument I can build is answered or explicitly bounded by the text.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by the perspective seat). This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications are accessible and defensible to readers from adjacent disciplines, and whether interdisciplinary claims (methods transferred from one field to another) are substantiated rather than asserted. I do not score it, but an analogy that does work in the argument it cannot bear is a coherence problem I will flag under D3.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by the journal-fit seat). This dimension asks whether organisation, clarity, figures and tables, and venue conventions are adequate. I do not score it; I note structural matters only where they obscure or fragment the line of argument.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the journal-fit seat). This dimension asks whether the work fits the target venue and makes an original, significant contribution for its readers. I do not score it; my "so what?" test feeds findings here without a score.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence

dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the headline thesis is entailed by the reported analyses rather than by analogy or renaming; whether every major claim traces to a specific result; whether sections and components support one single story or pull in different directions; whether rival explanations (measurement, recording, reporting and deployment processes; aggregation and power artefacts; confounding by omitted covariates) are named and tested or explicitly bounded; whether temporal, spatial and causal scope of the conclusions matches the data; whether any result reported elsewhere in the text contradicts a headline claim; whether any material is superfluous to the central story.
what_triggers_block: A headline or abstract-level claim that does not follow from the evidence shown, or that is directly contradicted by another result in the same manuscript, or that depends on a rival explanation being false without any test or bounding of that rival, but which could be repaired by reframing, additional analysis or narrowing of scope.
what_triggers_warn: Logic chain mostly holds but contains unacknowledged leaps, overstated scope, analogical language doing inferential work, internal tensions between sections that the text does not reconcile, or untested alternative explanations that weaken but do not overturn the central claim.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis is shown to be unsupportable by the paper's own evidence: either the data actively contradict it, or a more parsimonious rival explanation fits the reported results at least as well and the design cannot in principle discriminate between them, so no revision short of a different study could rescue the claim.
